## 2. Recorded lighting scenes

How Apex records them today (verified in the source):

- `.apexscene`: a Recording (`[ui] recorder_key`) in developer mode writes `Captures\<date> Recording\Lighting
  scene.apexscene` (`OutdoorDelta::SceneBegin`/`SceneEnd`, format `features/scene_record.h`, reader
  `tools/scene_replay`) when the physical indoor lamp model and the GPU room lamps capture are on.
- `World.txt` (story grids, placed floors, lights): **no writer exists in this repository.** Only the reader in
  `tools/rt_lamps_test` remains; the Full capture that wrote it is not in the public history. The Lighting snapshot
  has the lights but in another line format and no story grids. A World.txt writer is a separate package (it needs
  the story grid walk); until then S0-07 and R-02 can use `.apexscene` rooms or the synthetic stories.
- Night frame: Frame Capture (`[ui] frame_capture_key`, `[patches.FrameCapture]`) writes
  `ApexRadiance_FrameCapture.txt` beside the log (two frames, every draw with targets, shaders and textures).

Job `tools/ingame_runner/jobs/realtime-gi-scenes.json`: ApexTest-Town, clock moved to 22:00, a Lighting snapshot,
three Recordings of the household's lot from three camera places (one paused, two at normal speed), a filtered
screenshot and one Frame Capture. Bare F-key shortcuts (F6 recorder, F7 snapshot, F8 screenshot, F12 frame capture).
The `expect` lines require each part to be written and at least one room solve in a scene.

- **ApexTest-Night does not exist on this machine** (verified: Saves folder), so the job uses ApexTest-Town with a new
  runner step `time` (`{ "hour": 22 }`, agent command `time_set`: the game's `SimClock.AddOneHour` until the clock
  reads the hour). That the sky and window lights follow such a jump is inferred; the snapshot's night level line
  shows it.
